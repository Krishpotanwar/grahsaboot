## Problem Statement

The owner's report, 2026-10-08, verbatim: "there is a problem i just observed two things one: nothing can be interpreted from the image second without coordinates the user should be able to draw on the map but we are unable to draw it we need a more clear satellite image or we should be able to combine multiple images from satellite".

Diagnosis, from scratch scripts on a test build. Chromium only: reproduction in Firefox is an E0 task, WebKit cannot run on the build VM, and the owner's own browser, OS and device when drawing failed are not recorded (Open Questions). Two separate complaints:

1. **"We are unable to draw" looks like a legibility problem, not an event bug (unverified outside Chromium).** Drawing works mechanically (mouse, double-click, Enter, touch). What a person sees while drawing is the problem:
   - the dark OpenFreeMap style is near-black at street zoom (building fill `rgb(10,10,10)` is darker than the background `rgb(12,12,12)`, minor roads are about 1.1:1 against it, labels `rgb(80,78,78)`);
   - there is no satellite imagery at street zoom at all (z>=5 is vector only; the spec's optional "Context imagery (2016)" toggle was never built; probe P6 is pending). The owner chose the vector design (V1) over the imagery design (V2) at design time (`docs/design/README.md`); the 2026-10-08 report and D14 reverse part of that choice, for the outline step only and as an opt-in switch;
   - the attribution box starts open over the map, and the map is black for the first seconds with no word saying it is loading.
2. **"Nothing can be interpreted from the image" is the 10 m limit, not a bug.** The evidence photos are Sentinel-2 at 10 m per pixel: a 2 km airport site is readable (about 208 x 206 px), a single house is 1-3 pixels. A prototype of "clearer from the same data" (bicubic upscaling, joint 1-99.5 % stretch, mild unsharp) gave only a modest gain. Combining several scenes removes clouds and noise; it cannot add detail.

Landscape (web search 2026-10-08; untrusted, not independently verified): Bhuvan views to about 1 m but its free downloads are 23 m or coarser and its terms for product use are unclear (https://bhuvan.nrsc.gov.in/wiki/index.php/Free_Satellite_Data_Download); OpenAerialMap is CC BY 4.0 and high resolution only where contributors uploaded (https://openaerialmap.org/about/, https://registry.opendata.aws/openaerialmap/); ESA funds a feasibility study for monitoring developments over 10 ha and notes that visible change does not prove completed workmanship (https://business.esa.int/sites/business/files/Annex%20-%20Space%20for%20Construction%20Monitoring%20Use-Cases.pdf, https://newspaceeconomy.ca/2026/10/03/can-satellite-construction-monitoring-make-project-progress-easier-to-verify/). Narrowed claim: no free, licensable, wide-coverage imagery finer than 10 m for arbitrary places in India was found in that search.

Q2 answer: users today use "Copernicus Browser or a GIS person" (the spec's baseline). Nobody has observed this. ## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

Q3 answer: "I cannot name one person yet." Four groups, no individuals: the Nagpur civil-engineering network, CSR impact-assessment consultants, RTI and social-audit groups, local journalists (premise 3 said "three groups" as shorthand). CSR note: under the Companies (CSR Policy) Rules, as remembered and not verified here, impact assessment applies to companies with an average CSR obligation of Rs 10 crore or more, for projects of Rs 1 crore or more completed at least a year earlier. That is a before/after use after completion, so a CSR consultant joins the first five only with a completed project.

Wedge from the spec: a free, non-GIS, outline-in / evidence-report-out page for a large site or road corridor. Narrowest version worth testing first: sites big enough for 10 m to show (quarries, sand-mining stretches of river, lake edges, mine dumps, expressway stretches, large campuses), not single buildings.

## Constraints

- Free and open data only (spec D6); Sentinel-2 L2A from the same public COGs as the evidence; zero-rupee infrastructure; no new npm dependency.
- Context is not evidence: no hash, never in the report, provenance, verify function or saved frames (enforced by an allowlist guard, below).
- Honest limits stay the product's stance: nothing claims to show houses, and the satellite view is labelled "for finding your place, not evidence".
- Phase E starts only after the current plan finishes (Order of Work). The Phase D dormancy guard stays green.
- Tier 0 (no WebGL) keeps working through the coordinate forms.
- EOX Sentinel-2 cloudless: only the 2016 mosaic is CC BY 4.0; 2018-2025 are non-commercial (`docs/geoverify/research/2026-10-05-r3-frontend.md`, B1).

## Premises

1. **Sharpened by the second opinion.** For the "unable to draw" half, the wall is the front door: finding a place and seeing the ground well enough to outline it (a readable map; an orientation picture). For the "nothing can be interpreted" half, the wall is the 10 m limit: nothing free sharpens it, so it is answered by saying what 10 m can show for the shape just drawn and by aiming at sites big enough to see. The satellite view in approach B is an orientation aid for the front door, not a clearer evidence photo.
2. **Revised at D13.** Success is a named person using a report in a real case (an RTI appeal, a news story, an assessment). The rival is whatever people do today, found by asking ten named people; Copernicus Browser or a GIS person is the spec's assumption. "A non-GIS person goes from place to outline to report alone in one sitting" becomes the second test (Success Criteria). Original wording: the thing to beat is "needing a GIS person" and success means doing it alone in one sitting.
3. There is no demand evidence and no named user, so the optional parts (click-a-building, photo zoom and enhance, the multi-scene composite, any paid imagery) wait for The Gate.
4. Imagery stays free and Sentinel-2 only; a paid or keyed layer follows the Gate's last row.

## The Gate

Defined once; every other section points here.

| Event | Evidence | What it releases |
|---|---|---|
| Watched | one person from the four groups outlined their own site unaided while the owner watched, and named where they got stuck | the one deferred item their stuck point names (for example "I could not tell the buildings apart" points at photo zoom/enhance; "I could not find the place" at building pick) |
| Used | at least 2 of 5 reports used with proof within 21 days of sending | the second test, and the further deferred items the recipients' blockers name |
| One used | exactly 1 of 5 | nothing; send five more and re-score after another 21 days |
| None used | 0 of 5 after 21 days | stop imagery work (the satellite view and every deferred item not yet built; E1 and the size note are not imagery and stay); rethink who needs a report and why |
| Paid imagery | "Watched" or "Used", plus a recipient who names 10 m as the blocker in a real case, plus the owner's written provider, terms and key decision | task E6 only |

Proof of use means a filed appeal or application that cites the report, a published story or link, an assessment line the recipient can show, or a written message naming the case; "looks good" is interest, not use. The owner decides each release.

## Cross-Model Perspective

- **The tell:** "nothing can be interpreted from the image ... we need a more clear satellite image". The only thing anyone actually did with the product was judge the output, not the way in, and free data cannot supply what was asked for (combining scenes removes clouds, not blur). Build: tell the user what 10 m can see in their shape; recruit people whose sites are big enough to see.
- **Wrong premise: #2.** Nobody in these groups hires a GIS person; "alone in one sitting" cannot be measured with zero users (for the first ten you are the GIS person); the real test is whether a report gets used. Proof it was right: ask 10 named people what they used last time; make reports by hand for their own sites and see whether 2 of 5 use them and ask "can I do this myself?".
- **48 hours:** a readable light map in both themes; a "Satellite" toggle from EOX's hosted Sentinel-2 cloudless tiles (2016 is CC BY, later years CC BY-NC-SA, to recheck before charging); place search on Enter via Nominatim; a size check when the outline closes; day 2 with no code: hand-made reports to 5-10 named people. Skip: own composite, click-a-building, photo zoom, keyed backdrop, accounts. Note it, don't build for it: when the Sentinel-2 archive is reprocessed the bytes change, so a hash can only be re-checked while that exact file exists; say so on the report.
- **Recommendation:** cut Phase E to a one-day fix of the way in and put hand-made reports in front of named people.

Not accepted: "use EOX" (only the 2016 mosaic is licensable for a commercial product, so it is a ten-year-old picture). Already built: Nominatim search on Enter, and the reprocessing caveat (processing baseline plus `source_identity`, "Source file changed since capture"). ## Recommended Approach

**B, chosen by the owner at D14 (2026-10-09).**

**What B promises.** A recent 10 m orientation picture under the map, for finding a place. It does NOT make the evidence photos clearer; nothing free can. **G0:** before E2-lite and E3 are built, the owner looks at a real single-scene render (a P11 output) of the worked example and one village at z15-z17 and says yes or no; if no, B reduces to A plus the size note.

**In scope**
- **E0 (reduced):** P11 single-scene cost and render quality (pass rule below); P12 MapLibre 6.12 facts (image update route under the CSP, cost of toggling layers, collapsing the compact attribution, layer order for the image: below the first road layer if that removes the need to hide fills) and terra-draw 1.36's built-in mode-level undo; P13 style-drift probe; a Firefox reproduction of drawing (mouse and touch); an informational OpenAerialMap coverage check at the five assignment sites; a throttled-4G plus 4x-CPU run in Chromium as the proxy for a mid-range Android.
- **E1 readable map:** a per-theme paint patch table over the OpenFreeMap `dark` and `positron` styles (contrast unit-tested on vendored copies: roads and building outlines >= 3:1 against the background, label text >= 4.5:1 against its halo); "Loading the map" and "The map could not load. Enter coordinates instead." lines; attribution collapsed at start; hint "Click each corner of your site. Click the first corner again, double-click or press Enter to finish."; Undo and Finish buttons, 44 px targets, conditional on the P12 probe (Undo = terra-draw's built-in undo; Finish = built from the drawing snapshot, since terra-draw has no public finish call). If the probe fails, the buttons are not built and the hint says "Press Enter to finish; Esc cancels".
- **Size note (new):** site: when the polygon closes or is edited, pixels = area / 100 m2; road: when the line is finished and its width is set, pixels across = width / 10 m, plus length. It warns and never blocks. Below the 1,000 m2 floor the existing refusal message is shown instead of the note. Thresholds and words are an open item (about 1 ha for a site and about 20 m for a corridor were suggested).
- **E2-lite:** `regionFor` (<= 12 km box), `gridFor`, `levelFor`, `rankScenes`, and `loadContext` reading ONE scene through the existing worker ops. Ranking: region coverage >= 90 % first, then lowest cloud over the region, then newest. Window 75 days, extended in two steps (150, then 240 days) only when no scene reaches 80 % clear; never older than 240 days; the status line always says how old the scene is. No compositor interface until P11 shows it is needed.
- **E3 satellite view, outline step only (the workbench mini map is out of B):**
  - an opt-in "Map | Satellite" switch, default Map on every browser and connection (no connection sniffing: Safari and Firefox cannot report one), cost shown ("about 8 MB when on"), the choice remembered in `localStorage` (never required);
  - the switch and its live status line sit in a row above the map at every width, never floating over the drawing area; "Update for this view" and "Cancel";
  - the image goes under roads and labels, drawn with MapLibre's default linear resampling and no sharpening (soft at z15-z17 by nature: a 10 m pixel is about 7 screen px at z17); it covers only its region; screenshots at z13, z15 and z17 in both themes go in the E3 report;
  - the attribution control, collapsed at start, lists OSM/OpenFreeMap and "Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data <year>" whenever the satellite view is on (tested).
- **Guard (allowlist):** only modules under `src/map/` and `src/new/` may import `src/context/`; no SHA identifier in `src/context/`; a test that photo/frame export and the report never read the context layer.
- **E7:** Limits-page copy (a house is 1-3 pixels; the view is an orientation aid), README row, probe rows; it verifies that the false accounts sentence is gone from `/limits` (D8a).

**Deferred behind The Gate:** multi-scene compositor and band matching (E2b), building pick (E4, probe P10), photo viewer zoom/pan/Enhance (E5; the plan had it inside Phase E, which contradicted premise 3), the satellite view on the workbench mini map, the keyed high-resolution backdrop (E6, owner-gated).

**Wireframe:** `docs/designs/phase-e-outline-sketch.png` (source `.html` beside it). The owner approved it as "good enough" (D15, 2026-10-09); the switch row above the map at every width was decided afterwards in the design review.

| Satellite state | Words (status line under the switch) |
|---|---|
| Off | "Satellite view is off (about 8 MB when on). Turn it on to see a recent photo behind the map." |
| Loading | "Finding the clearest recent photo... about 3 of 8 MB." + Cancel |
| On | "Satellite view · Sentinel-2 · 14 Sep 2026 · 10 m · for finding your place, not evidence." + Update for this view |
| Older scene | the same line with "12 Mar 2026 (7 months ago) ... newer photos were cloudy" |
| All cloudy | "Every recent photo of this area is cloudy. Showing the clearest one (14 Aug 2026, 62% clear). Try Update later, or use the map." |
| Nothing usable | "No recent photo covers this area. The map still works; draw on it as usual." |
| Offline | "Could not reach the photo service. The map still works." + Try again |

Size-note words (examples): "About 4,200 pixels at 10 m. Large buildings, roads and land clearing will show at this size." / "About 150 pixels at 10 m. Single buildings and narrow lanes will not show; expect 'not enough clear view'. You can still continue." / "12 m wide is about 1 pixel across. At 10 m a narrow road is mixed with what is beside it."

**Plan deltas** (applied to `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-08-grahsaboot-phase-e-clear-imagery.md` right after approval, before the engineering review): E0 shrinks to P11 (with its pass rule), P12, P13, the Firefox drawing check, the OpenAerialMap check and the throttled run (P10 moves with E4); E1 gains the conditional Undo/Finish rule and the Copernicus attribution test; a new size-note task; E2 becomes the single-scene loader and the compositor becomes E2b behind The Gate; E3 is the outline step only, opt-in everywhere, controls row above the map, label "Satellite view · Sentinel-2 · <date> · 10 m · ..." ## Success Criteria

**Product, first test (premise 2):** ask ten named people what they used last time; send five hand-made reports; at least 2 of 5 recipients use theirs in a real case, with proof, within 21 days of sending, and at least one asks "can I do this myself?". The Gate table holds every outcome's action; "used but nobody asks" means keep serving people by hand and build only report-quality fixes.

**Product, second test (runs only after "Used"):** five people who have not seen the tool outline their own site alone on their own device, with no coaching beyond "outline the place you care about". Pass: at least 3 of 5 reach a closed outline and the report preview within 15 minutes with no help from the owner (any hint counts as a fail for that person).

**Acceptance of E1 and E3 (scripted):** an unaided journey (place by name, readable map, closed outline, size note, Next) at 390 px and 1440 px in both themes; a test for each state above (loading line, failure line, collapsed attribution, hint, Undo/Finish or the fallback hint, and every satellite state in the table); the owner's G0 session on the worked example and one village.

**Engineering gates** (time and byte numbers are provisional until P11 replaces them with single-scene measurements and the throttled run):
- from switching on to the picture on the map <= 15 s on the worked example (build VM, real services, cold cache); <= 12 MB at level 0 (<= 6 MB at level 1 for regions above 10 km); 0 long tasks over 50 ms; <= 80 MB extra memory; one load in flight per map; everything aborts on switch-off, place change and unmount.
- Entry JS <= 150 KB gzip (the context code is lazy).
- Contrast of the patched maps as in E1 (unit test on vendored styles).
- The allowlist guard and the export test pass; the size note appears for every closed outline and never blocks; the 1,000 m2 refusal is unchanged.
- The Phase D dormancy guard and `dormant.spec.ts` stay green; axe clean in both themes on every screen touched.

### R1-1 — completeness

**Problem**

> The diagnosis 'Drawing works mechanically' and the conclusion 'We are unable to draw is a front-door problem we can fix' (Problem Statement 1, Premise 1) rest on scratch scripts in Chromium only ('Safari and Firefox not tried'). The owner's own browser, OS and device when they could not draw are not recorded, so a WebKit, Firefox or touch-specific failure would survive E1 unnoticed and the cause stays an inference.

**Remedy**

> Record the owner's browser/OS/device, reproduce the drawing flow (mouse and touch) on WebKit and Firefox in E0, and either confirm 'visibility only' or add the engine-specific fix to E1. Until then mark the conclusion unverified.

### R1-2 — completeness

**Problem**

> Success Criteria cover timing, memory, bundle size, contrast, the import guard, the size note, dormancy and axe only. Nothing checks the owner's actual complaint (a first-time person finds a place and outlines it unaided, dark and light, at phone width) or the committed E1/E3 behaviors (hint, Undo/Finish, loading and failure lines, collapsed attribution, all-cloudy / nothing usable / offline / Save-Data states). How the 10 m image behaves at street zoom (resampling, maximum zoom or fade at z15-z18) is never specified, yet that is what the owner will judge.

**Remedy**

> Add a task-based acceptance (unaided outline of the worked example and one village on the new build) and a gate per committed E1/E3 state; state the context image's resampling and zoom limits and review screenshots at z15-z18 in both themes.

### R1-3 — completeness

**Problem**

> E3's label ('Satellite view | Sentinel-2 | <date> | 10 m | for finding your place, not evidence') and E1's 'attribution collapsed at start' never say that the new layer adds 'Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data <years>', which the plan's Global Constraints require for the resampled, stretched imagery, or that the credit stays reachable once the control is collapsed.

**Remedy**

> Add the Copernicus credit to E3 scope and a test that the collapsed attribution control lists the OSM/OpenFreeMap and Copernicus credits whenever the satellite view is on.

### R1-4 — completeness

**Problem**

> E3 states only 'default OFF on Save-Data, 2g and 3g'. The default everywhere else is never stated (the plan makes it ON for tiers 2-3), and the repo's R3 research (section 4.4, Q5) says saveData and effectiveType are Chromium-only, with Safari and Firefox starting at tier 2. Those browsers on mobile data would auto-fetch up to the 12 MB budget at the outline step, before any demand evidence exists.

**Remedy**

> State the default for unmetered and for undetectable connections. Shortest safe rule: opt-in everywhere (OFF, cost shown), which also removes the connection sniffing; if default-ON stays, make unknown connection mean OFF.

### R1-5 — completeness

**Problem**

> Premise 2 says the original success test ('a non-GIS person goes from place to outline to report alone in one sitting') 'becomes the second test, run after someone has used a hand-made report', but Success Criteria define only the first test. The second test has no pass rule, sample, trigger or session design (the Assignment's sit-down only collects 'the first place they got stuck').

**Remedy**

> Add the second test to Success Criteria (who, how many, what counts as unaided success, when it runs) or state that it is defined after the first result.

### R1-6 — completeness

**Problem**

> The product test 'at least two [of five] are used in a real case' has no time window and no proof standard. Without a deadline the 'none of the five is used' branch can never fire, and self-reported use is easy to overstate when the owner is the one asking.

**Remedy**

> Set a fixed window from the day of sending and a minimum proof of use per recipient (a filed appeal, a published link, an assessment line they can show).

### R1-7 — consistency

**Problem**

> The test population changes by section. The Assignment makes 'three reports' for three named people; its decision rule and Success Criteria score 'two of five'; Status Quo and Premise 2 find the rival by asking 'ten named people'; Open Questions ask for 'the first ten named people'. Three reports can never be scored as five, and nothing says how three, five and ten relate.

**Remedy**

> Choose the numbers and their order (for example ask ten, send five) and use them identically in the Assignment, Success Criteria, Status Quo and Open Questions.

### R1-8 — consistency

**Problem**

> The gate for deferred work is defined four ways: Premise 3 'one real person from the three groups has tried the outline step'; the Deferred list 'a real person used a report and was blocked by this'; the Assignment 'two of five use a report and ask can I do this myself'; Premise 4 'a real tester says 10 m is the blocker' (while Premise 3 also lists 'any paid imagery' behind its own gate). One person versus two of five, and 'tried the outline step' versus 'used a report', would release different work.

**Remedy**

> Define one gate (who, how many, what evidence, who decides) in one place and reference it from Premises 3 and 4, the Deferred list and the Assignment; give paid imagery a single trigger.
