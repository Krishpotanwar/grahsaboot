# GeoVerify science and data revalidation

Checked: **5 October 2026**. Status: research and proposed design changes; no imagery processing, trained model, benchmark, or account-specific capability test was performed. Existing GeoVerify documents and the uploaded files were used to identify claims, then checked against primary sources afresh. They were not treated as evidence that a claim is true.

## Findings that change the plan

1. **Exclude LEVIR-CD from the commercial data path unless separate permission is established.** Its own README restricts images and annotations to academic use and explicitly prohibits commercial use. The older notebook recorded its resolution but missed this restriction. Free access to GeoVerify does not remove its stated commercial intent. Dataset, code, and pretrained-checkpoint rights need separate records. [Dataset owner's terms](https://github.com/justchenhao/LEVIR/blob/master/README.md)
2. **Use actual dated observations as the primary evidence.** Recommend a timeline of selected acquisitions, with missing observations left visible. Monthly bins can organise the timeline; they need not create a synthetic monthly image. A median composite answers a different question and should be an optional, clearly marked derived view. This is a design recommendation, not a measured superiority claim.
3. **Add processing-baseline and edge-quality checks.** ESA activated Sentinel-2 Processing Baseline 05.13 on 21 September 2026. Current product documentation says it fixes an artificial-pixel issue near swath edges; older products need additional care. Merely masking SCL/clouds is not a complete quality contract. [Activation notice](https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/-/activation-of-copernicus-sentinel-2-processing-baseline-05.13-on-21-september-2026), [product limitations](https://sentiwiki.copernicus.eu/web/s2-products)
4. **Keep roads, but change what is promised.** Corridor inspection and dated evidence are plausible month-one outputs. Pavement width, completed road length, connectivity and contractual completion require different reference data and validation. No retrieved source establishes reliable performance for GeoVerify's proposed road sizes in India.
5. **The broad novelty claim is untenable as a starting assumption.** ESA already describes construction-progress monitoring and publishes an example of new-road change detection using Sentinel-2. These do not prove an identical product exists, but they invalidate treating “research stops at masks” as established fact. Position the product around usable evidence and its eventual validated customer workflow. [ESA construction programme](https://business.esa.int/funding/call-for-proposals-non-competitive/space-for-construction-monitoring), [ESA transport infrastructure brochure](https://gda.esa.int/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/ESA-TI-Brochure-2.pdf)

## Three science approaches compared

The following is a recommendation for design review. All options retain managed processing plus an evidence workbench, both buildings/sites and roads, before/after and timeline, free/open source imagery, and the one-month initial target.

| Option | What it produces | Benefit | Cost or limitation | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S1. Selected real observations** | Small, dated set of acquisitions, quality masks, aligned previews, source metadata, user notes | Directly inspectable temporal evidence; simpler explanation of which image supports which observation | Cloud gaps remain; selection and site-level quality evaluation consume requests too | **Month-one default** |
| **S2. Composite and index change** | Monthly/seasonal derived images and experimental surface-change overlays | May increase usable spatial coverage; useful later for broad surface patterns | Pixel dates differ, short events can be obscured, indices confuse materials; extra provenance and validation | Optional research branch after S1 works |
| **S3. Optical plus radar** | Separate Sentinel-2 and Sentinel-1 evidence, eventually evaluated fusion | Potential continuity during monsoon/cloud-heavy periods | Added scattering/geometry expertise, preprocessing, cost and labels; a radar change is not a construction verdict | Targeted second-phase experiment only |

ESA's managed openEO catalogue already includes a Sentinel-1 change process using multiple acquisitions, orbit separation and statistical/AI methods, but its stated application is potential **forest disturbance**. Reuse its processing ideas only after checking licence, execution cost and target fit; do not relabel its output as construction. [ESA APEx algorithm](https://algorithm-catalogue.apex.esa.int/apps/sentinel1_changedetection)

## Recommended month-one evidence pipeline

This is a proposed implementation contract, not measured performance.

1. **Confirm geometry and purpose.** A point helps locate a site; an explicit polygon or buffered route defines the inspected area. Keep the user's claimed road length/width distinct from the analysis buffer. Display the source resolution and the object dimensions entered by the user without claiming a minimum detectable size.
2. **Discover before processing.** Search metadata for actual acquisitions in requested periods. Record coverage, product/baseline, timestamp, orbit/tile and provider identity. Bound candidates and total work before expensive calls. A low tile-wide cloud fraction only ranks candidates; it does not pass site quality.
3. **Evaluate local visibility.** Fetch or compute site-level masks under the provider allowance. Treat nodata, cloud/shadow, snow and known edge problems explicitly. Do not call all clear pixels “construction pixels”. Preserve a useful partially clear observation with its mask if the relevant part is visible; otherwise return a gap. Numeric acceptance thresholds need experiments.
4. **Select real observations.** Pick a bounded representative image per display bin, while allowing inspection of alternatives. Prefer suitable temporal separation and comparable conditions. Record the actual date and selection reason. Never substitute a later image for “as of deadline” evidence without visibly changing the question. Adjacent tiles from the same acquisition may be mosaicked with each contributor retained; different days must not masquerade as one photo.
5. **Render consistently.** Same projected grid, resolution, extent and RGB stretch across the comparison; retain units, harmonisation and mask settings. Avoid per-image auto-contrast making a visual difference appear larger. Persist the scientific/source metadata separately from 8-bit display pixels.
6. **Show evidence before interpretation.** Before/after slider, actual-date timeline, visible gaps, masks, source IDs and limitations. Building annotations concern visible exterior changes. Road annotations concern named route sections and observable corridor/surface changes. A user note records author and date and remains a user interpretation.
7. **Express timing as an interval.** If a visible change is absent in one suitable observation and present in a later one, report that it first becomes visible between those observation dates. Do not invent the exact construction start/finish day. Clouds widen the interval.
8. **Export an evidence packet.** Include actual images, masks, parameters, source links/IDs, requested and actual dates, attribution, notes and content hashes. Hashes protect the saved packet against unnoticed changes; they do not certify that a construction claim is true.

## Implementation experiments and release evidence

| Experiment | What to record | Passing meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Paired acquisition provenance | Requested interval, chosen scene IDs/times, contributing tiles, manifest and output hash | Displayed dates and images demonstrably match; no accidental cross-day mosaic |
| Baseline and units | Collection/baseline, provider units/offset/harmonisation, identical test region | Contract explains numerical values and avoids double correction; not a guarantee that all seasons match |
| Quality failure set | Cloud, fog, edge, shadow, nodata, partial coverage and stable boundaries | Missing/invalid evidence is visible and cannot silently become no-change |
| Buildings and road corridors | Independent dated reference, geometry, dimensions, actual imagery and reviewer notes | Establishes what can be inspected in each sample; unsupported samples retained |
| Date selection | Endpoint, missing month, overlapping tiles, alternate candidate, cross-season case | No silent deadline substitution; exact selected observations can be reproduced |
| Resource envelope | Candidates inspected, processing requests/credits, bytes, elapsed time and cache effects | Informs admission limits; does not infer jobs/month from source allowance alone |
| Automated overlay, if attempted | Frozen labels, grouped split, per-class errors, abstentions, reviewers independent of predictions | Separate evidence for release; fail or defer without blocking an honestly labelled workbench |

Do not expand a 24-case exploratory set into thousands of supposedly independent tests by cropping patches. Do not interpret a successful hand-picked demo as customer validation. The first month can deliver live comparative evidence while still withholding unsupported construction labels or quantities.

## Remaining unverified points

- Actual India-first site/date coverage, including monsoon, and usable support for selected building dimensions and road widths.
- Whether the selected managed processor exposes/applies the required historical edge masks and complete acquisition provenance.
- Commercial terms for the exact OSCD/WHU datasets and any candidate weights; LEVIR's restrictive published terms are confirmed.
- Residual registration and radiometric effects on intended measurements; real cost of scene selection versus composites.
- Construction-class calibration, false alarms/misses, reliable physical quantities, exact project outcomes, user demand and time savings.
- Exhaustive novelty or market coverage. Existing initiatives establish related work, not GeoVerify demand or an identical competing product.

These are deliberately marked as unverified. More web research cannot replace the site-level experiments or independent reference evidence.
